First fork release, based on upstream 1.5.14.

File storage is now folder-authoritative:
• Notes are read from and written directly to the folder you select.
• Files moved or renamed by another app are followed; notes are not duplicated or recreated.
• Files deleted or moved out of the folder remove their notes from the app.
• External edits win; ambiguous files are left alone instead of being overwritten.
• Unsaved local edits are kept if a note's file disappears.
• Nested folders are scanned; hidden folders, .trash and sync-conflict files are ignored.
• Failed writes are retried, and a confirmed "Rescan folder" action applies the folder's current state.
• An empty or unavailable folder never triggers a mass deletion.
• Nextcloud sync is unchanged.

Under the hood:
• Notes are matched by relative path and content hash.
• Duplicate note mappings are prevented by a database migration and unique index.
• Stale writes can no longer recreate deleted files.
• Filenames are sanitized so notes cannot become hidden or sync-conflict files.
